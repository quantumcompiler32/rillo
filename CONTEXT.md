# Rillo planning

Rillo helps a Milpitas Middle College student organize college enrollment and estimate progress toward college credentials.

## Language

**Course**:
A college catalog offering identified by a course code and an owning college. It may be offered in many terms and sections.

**Planned class**:
A student's intention to take a course in a particular term. It is not proof of approval, registration, enrollment, or earned credit.
_Avoid_: Completed course

**Enrollment**:
A student's confirmed registration in a particular class section. It is distinct from a plan and from R-40 approval.

**Completed course**:
A course with an official outcome recorded by its college. Whether it satisfies a credential requirement depends on the applicable catalog and evaluation rules.

**R-40 approval**:
The special-admit authorization for a high school student to enroll in specified college courses for a term. College processing and any prerequisite clearance are separate from registration.
_Avoid_: R40 degree requirement

**Degree goal**:
A credential the student is exploring or working toward, such as an AA or AS-T. It is not an awarded degree.

**Requirement**:
A rule from a specific college's program and catalog year that may be satisfied by an eligible course, credit, or other milestone.

**Degree progress estimate**:
Rillo's explanation of which requirements appear fulfilled, planned, or unresolved based on the data and rules available. It is not an official degree audit.

**Cross-college credit candidate**:
A course completed or planned at one college that might satisfy a requirement at another. It remains pending until the degree-awarding college accepts the application of that credit.

**Transfer articulation**:
A published agreement describing how a course from one institution applies at another, often for transfer to a university. It is distinct from acceptance toward a community college's own degree.
