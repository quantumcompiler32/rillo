# College planning source notes — 2026-10-30

These sources inform product rules. Verify them against the applicable college and catalog year before publishing degree calculations.

## Enrollment

- SJCC says high school students submit a new application and R-40 each term; they may register only for courses listed on the form. R-40 processing, prerequisite clearance, and actual registration are distinct steps. [SJCC Dual Enrollment](https://sjcc.edu/students/academic-programs/dual-enrollment.aspx), [SJCC Concurrent Enrollment](https://sjcc.edu/milpitas-extension/milpitas-concurrent-enrollment.aspx).
- Mission uses its own Dual Enrollment Form, with parent/guardian and school counselor signatures, followed by processing, prerequisite clearance, and registration in My Mission Portal. Do not call Mission's form an R-40. [Mission Dual Enrollment](https://missioncollege.edu/office-of-instruction/educational-partnerships/dual-enrollment.html).

## Degree and course data

- SJCC publishes a 2026–27 catalog, major sheets, and a term class schedule. Its course numbering is changing under California common course numbering. [SJCC catalog](https://catalog.sjcc.edu/), [SJCC major sheets](https://sjcc.edu/counseling/major-sheets.aspx), [SJCC schedule](https://www.sjcc.edu/admissions-records/schedule.aspx).
- Mission publishes a 2026–27 catalog and a class search. Its public academics pages list Mathematics 2.0 AS-T and Physics 2.0 AS-T. [Mission academic services](https://missioncollege.edu/office-of-instruction/academic-services/), [Mission class schedule](https://missioncollege.edu/class_schedule/index.html/class_schedule/), [Mission mathematics](https://missioncollege.edu/academics/math/), [Mission physics](https://missioncollege.edu/academics/physics/).
- ASSIST explicitly excludes community-college-to-community-college transfer information. Use it for university transfer planning, not to certify that a Mission course satisfies an SJCC degree or vice versa. [ASSIST FAQ](https://resource.assist.org/FAQ).

## Product implications

- Distinguish course catalog entries, section offerings, a student's plan, special-admit approval, registration, and completed credit.
- Tie degree rules to awarding college and catalog year. Show source and verification state for each cross-college match.
- Do not turn a possible match into an earned degree claim.

| Question | San José City College | Mission College |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accreditation | ACCJC accredited ([source](https://www.sjcc.edu/academic-affairs/accreditation/default.aspx)) | ACCJC accredited ([source](https://missioncollege.edu/about/accreditation/)) |

| Physics AS-T | Not found in the 2026–27 degree list reviewed; confirm with SJCC before treating it as unavailable ([catalog](https://catalog.sjcc.edu/)) | Public program page lists Physics 2.0 AS-T ([program](https://missioncollege.edu/academics/physics/)) |
| What this supports | A Mathematics AS-T path at the student's Middle College partner | A single-college route to both named AS-T goals, subject to detailed catalog and credit review |

There is no sound evidence here that one college's name is intrinsically more credible to a transfer university or employer. Program fit, completed requirements, grades, official transcripts, and transfer agreements are better decision criteria. This comparison is not a recommendation to switch colleges without checking residency, catalog rights, course availability, and how existing credits would be evaluated.
